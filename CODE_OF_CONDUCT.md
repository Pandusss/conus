# Code of conduct

Conus is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. Be the kind of
collaborator you would want on your own project:

- be respectful and assume good intent; criticise ideas and code, never people;
- no harassment, insults, discrimination or unwelcome sexual attention, in any form or channel;
- keep discussions on topic and help newcomers find their way.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block people who
keep breaking them. Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub (a private security advisory
works too) — reports are handled confidentially.
